# ADR-000: Template

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: List of decision owners (e.g., Platform Engineering)

## Context

The forces at play. Why this decision is needed, what the constraints are, and what problem it solves. Write for a reader who has context on the platform but not on this specific decision.

## Decision

The decision itself, stated unambiguously. Include enough technical detail that someone could implement it from this record alone.

## Consequences

- **Positive**: what improves as a result.
- **Negative**: what this decision trades away, and any follow-up work it implies.
- **Positive/Negative**: repeat as needed.

## Alternatives

- **Alternative considered**: why it was rejected.
- **Another alternative**: why it was rejected.

---

Guidance: keep each section short. If a section exceeds ~10 lines, the decision is probably too large and should be split.
